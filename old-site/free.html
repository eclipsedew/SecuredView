Why it is free

A free VPN usually costs you something else.

Data sold on, advertising pushed in, or your connection quietly rented out to somebody else. Since we are asking you to believe none of that applies here, the least we can do is show the arithmetic.

The short answer

The capacity was already bought

SecuredView is one part of a network business that sells transit and colocation to companies. That business pays for the racks, the power, the transit contracts and the people who keep it running. It buys capacity in large blocks because that is how transit is priced, and large blocks are never fully used.

The spare portion is the product. Giving it away costs us the marginal bandwidth and a few engineers, which is a rounding error against what the network already earns. That is the entire model, and it is the only reason a free service can hold this position without needing something back from you.

Why bother at all

Two honest reasons. The first is that a well run public service is the best advertisement a network business can buy, and the people who evaluate our paid transit are the same people who notice whether the free thing is any good. The second is that most of us grew up somewhere the open internet was not a settled question, and this is the part of the job that feels worth doing.

Neither reason requires your data. If either ever did, this page would be a lie and you would be right to leave.

The transit and colocation business bills roughly 2.1 million dollars a month from paying customers. SecuredView is about a fifth of that, and it uses capacity those customers have already caused us to buy. It is a real cost and it is a survivable one, which is the point of showing you both numbers.

Commitments

Six things we will not do.

Written plainly so that breaking one would be obvious rather than arguable.

  • We will not sell traffic records, browsing data, or anything derived from either, to anyone, at any price
  • We will not insert advertising, trackers or affiliate redirects into the pages you load
  • We will not resell your connection as an exit point for anyone else's traffic
  • We will not slow you down to make a paid tier look better, because we will not build a paid tier
  • We will not add an account requirement, since a profile we cannot build is a profile nobody can demand
  • We will not take investment that arrives with a condition to change any of the five above

If it stops working

What happens if the funding goes

The transit business could lose customers, or the economics of bandwidth could move against us. If the free service stops being something we can carry, we will say so on this page and in the apps, keep running for ninety days while you move, and then shut it down.

What we will not do is keep it running by changing what it is. Selling the user base, turning on advertising, or introducing the paid tier we said we would not build are all worse outcomes than closing, because each of them would betray people who took us at our word on the strength of this page.

Can I pay you anyway

No, and thank you for asking. Taking money would create a class of people we owe something extra to, and the moment that class exists the service starts being shaped around it. If you want to help, run the client on your own hardware, tell us when something breaks, and read the audit when it is published.
