Pricing

Simple, transparent pricing.

The short answer

What you get

Thirteen server locations worldwide with full ChaCha20 Poly1305 encryption, no logs, and no ads. Every plan includes up to three devices and priority speeds. Plans start at $3.99 for 30 days, with 60-day ($7) and 90-day ($13) options available.

Why paid only

Running memory-only nodes in owned hardware across 13 countries costs real money. Charging for the service means we answer to our users, not to advertisers or data brokers. The pricing is simple because the business model is simple.

Commitments

Six things we will not do.

Written plainly so that breaking one would be obvious rather than arguable.

  • We will not sell traffic records, browsing data, or anything derived from either, to anyone, at any price
  • We will not insert advertising, trackers or affiliate redirects into the pages you load
  • We will not resell your connection as an exit point for anyone else's traffic
  • We will not throttle any plan to push you toward a more expensive one
  • We will not add unnecessary account requirements. A profile we cannot build is a profile nobody can demand
  • We will not take investment that arrives with a condition to change any of the five above

If it stops working

What happens if the funding goes

The business could lose customers, or the economics could move against us. If the service stops being something we can sustain, we will say so on this page and in the apps, keep running for ninety days while you move, and then shut it down.

What we will not do is keep it running by changing what it is. Selling the user base, turning on advertising, or degrading the service to force upgrades are all worse outcomes than closing, because each of them would betray people who took us at our word on the strength of this page.

How do I pay

Plans are available through the app and paid via Paystack. If you want to support us beyond that, run the client on your own hardware, tell us when something breaks, and read the audit when it is published.
